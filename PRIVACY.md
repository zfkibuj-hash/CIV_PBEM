# Privacy Policy: Civ4 PBEM Manager

**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

Civ4 PBEM Manager is a desktop application. It does **not** send analytics, crash reports, or usage data to the project authors.

## What stays on your computer

- Application settings and game list (local config)
- Optional master password protecting encrypted transport credentials (FTP/SFTP/email passwords) on disk
- Local copies of Civilization IV save files you choose to download or watch

## What leaves your computer

Only what **you** configure:

- Uploads/downloads of game saves and small state files to **your** chosen transport (FTP, SFTP, WebDAV, or email)
- Optional email notifications you enable (SMTP)
- Optional in-app notify flags uploaded to that same transport

The application never phones home to GitHub, SignPath, or the authors except when **you** use "Check for updates" (then it contacts GitHub Releases for version info).

## Third parties

- **GitHub Releases**: only if you check for updates or download the app
- **Your FTP/email host**: only with credentials you enter
- **SignPath** (if releases are signed): signing happens on their infrastructure for published builds; they do not receive your game data or passwords
